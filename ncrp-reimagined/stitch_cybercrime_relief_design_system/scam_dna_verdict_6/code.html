Part-time task scam

94% match to this script

Stage 3: Withdrawal blocked

The balance shown in the app is not real money.

IN WHAT YOU SENT

"Pay Rs 1500 to unlock your merchant task..."

"Join our VIP Telegram group"

"Sender ID: @HR_Recruitment_Official"

Next they will ask for a GST or unlocking fee to release the balance. There is no final payment that unlocks it.

Do not

  • Pay any tax, GST, or unlocking fee.
  • Delete the chat — it is your evidence.
  • Share an OTP or your screen with anyone who called you.

Real employers never ask for a deposit before paying you. Look the company up yourself, not through their link.
